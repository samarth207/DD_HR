# DD_HR

## Downloadable Payslips

- Employees can select a month under **Salary Breakup** and click **Download Payslip** after that month's salary is marked paid. The download is a single A4 PDF matching the supplied sample: DegreeDrishti header and logo, two-column employee details, side-by-side earnings/deductions, net pay in words, and the system-generated disclaimer.
- Admin/HR can record optional Gender, Date of Birth, PAN, Location and Unit under Employees. Missing or untracked details (including arrear days) display `-`, never sample employee data. Newly paid payroll snapshots these details; older paid records use current profile details with a note.
- PDFs read saved paid payroll only. Full-month components are earnings; joining-month proration and recorded loss of pay are deductions. Incentives and daily bonuses are additional earnings. Payable days follow the existing 30-day payroll basis, and LWP includes unpaid leave and late half days. This is presentation only; payroll amounts are unchanged.
- The PDF deduction section shows **Loss of Pay / LWP** using the saved LOP amount (or saved unpaid-leave plus late deductions in older records), and **TOTAL DEDUCTIONS** includes this amount once, plus any other recorded deductions and joining-month proration. Zero recorded LOP is shown as `0.00`; inconsistent saved deduction amounts require HR reconciliation.
- Paid records without a component snapshot show their saved gross without inventing a historical split. Incomplete or inconsistent saved payment amounts produce an explicit reconciliation error instead of an inaccurate payslip. Downloads cannot be used for unpaid periods or another employee's payroll, and responses are not cached.
- Install backend dependencies with `npm install --prefix server` and restart the backend after deployment. PDF generation runs locally with PDFKit; no external PDF service or browser print dialog is used. The supplied sample PDF is a design reference, not a runtime dependency.

## Salary Changes

- Admins enter **monthly CTC (INR)** when adding an employee. Basic Salary (50%), House Rent Allowance (20%), and Special Allowance (30%) are calculated automatically. Sub Total / Gross Salary and CTC equal the entered amount; each yearly amount is its monthly amount multiplied by 12. Amounts support two decimal places; any paise rounding remainder is assigned to Special Allowance so the components always sum to CTC.
- The full-month component table is shown in the employee Salary Breakup view (respecting its amount visibility toggle), management payroll details, and salary-credit emails. It is a salary structure, not additional earnings: existing joining-month proration, incentives, leave/attendance deductions, and net-pay calculations are unchanged. Annual figures are not year-to-date earnings. The table omits the annualization/taxability guidance paragraph.
- Existing employees keep their salary and have no component breakup until an admin re-enters their monthly CTC and selects an effective month. Recording the structure without changing the total is supported. Subsequent CTC revisions retain the automatic split. Effective-dated history and paid snapshots preserve the structure applicable to each period; older paid slips without recorded components are explicitly marked as unrecorded, not retroactively recalculated.
- Change salary through Employees > Edit Employee, then select the effective month and optionally enter a reason.
- Changes apply from the first day of the selected month, including future months. Future entries are marked Scheduled, while profiles and payroll continue using the currently effective salary until that month. Multiple changes in the same month are not supported. Effective months must follow the previous change and cannot overlap any paid payroll period.
- A position entered alongside a salary change is saved in its history entry and shown in both history views. Position edits themselves take effect immediately; only the salary is future-dated.
- Salary changes retain the previous rate, effective date, department, designation, reason, recording time, and management identity in the employee's `salaryHistory`. Ordinary profile edits cannot replace this history.
- Employees can view these records under Salary Breakup > Salary Changes. Amounts are hidden until the visibility button is selected.
- Admin and HR can open Employees > employee row > Salary History (history icon) to view effective dates, previous/new salary, change percentages, department, designation, reason, and recording details.
- Payroll previews and salary-till-date use the applicable historical rate. Newly paid payroll stores a complete breakup snapshot so later changes do not recalculate paid salary.
- Existing employees require no migration: the first recorded change preserves their current salary as the initial rate. Rates overwritten before this feature cannot be reconstructed automatically; reconcile them from verified historical records. Existing payments without a breakup snapshot retain their stored gross salary but cannot recover missing historical calculation details.
- Restart the backend after deployment. Payroll regression checks: `npx --prefix server jest --runInBand --config server/jest.qa.config.js --runTestsByPath server/tests/integration/qa.integration.test.js --testNamePattern "Payroll|Salary"`.
